1. Description of the Service

Daily Replay is an automated, private reflection diary and digital wellbeing journal. The App analyzes on-device application usage metrics (via Android Usage Access) and physical movement (via Android Health Connect) to synthesize daily timeline reflections, mood tracking, and visual story cards.
